It reads like you wrote it.

You say

hi Sam um so I had a look at the draft and it's really good, I just have a couple of small notes, uh, can we go through them on Wednesday, no wait, Thursday afternoon, thanks, Jo

Speak types

Hi Sam,

I had a look at the draft and it's really good. I just have a couple of small notes. Can we go through them on Thursday afternoon?

Thanks,
Jo

  • Ums and uhs, gone.
  • Change your mind mid-sentence. Speak keeps the final version.
  • Punctuation, capitals and line breaks, added.
  • Names and terms from your dictionary, spelled right.
