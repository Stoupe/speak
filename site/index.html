Say it messy. Send it clean.

Ramble, say um, change your mind halfway. Speak turns it into tidy, punctuated text, right on your Mac. Free, private and open source.

Free. No account needed.

  • Free

    No subscription, no word limits, no account. The models already come with your Mac.

  • Private

    Speech recognition and cleanup run on your Mac. Nothing goes to the cloud. Audio is never saved.

  • Everywhere

    Slack, Mail, Notes, your code editor. If you can type there, you can talk there.

It reads like you wrote it.

You say

hi Sam um so I had a look at the draft and it's really good, I just have a couple of small notes, uh, can we go through them on Wednesday, no wait, Thursday afternoon, thanks, Jo

Speak types

Hi Sam,

I had a look at the draft and it's really good. I just have a couple of small notes. Can we go through them on Thursday afternoon?

Thanks,
Jo

  • Ums and uhs, gone.
  • Change your mind mid-sentence. Speak keeps the final version.
  • Punctuation, capitals and line breaks, added.
  • Names and terms from your dictionary, spelled right.

One key. That's it.

fn hold
Talk. Let go to paste.
fn fn
Hands-free. Press fn again to finish.
esc
Cancel.

No fn key? Use right ⌥ option or right ⌘ command.
